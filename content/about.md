Hi, I'm Leah.

This site is a record of my life: the ordinary days, the projects, the places, the hobbies, and the people. I'm not trying to build an audience. I just want something to remain that says who I was and what my life was like, for anyone who wonders later.

It's also a reason to write again, which I've missed.

If you've found your way here, years from now or next week, welcome.
